## Scene 1: The Problem & The Hook (0:00 – 0:45)

### 🖥️ Screen Action:
- Open your browser to the **Landing Page** (`http://localhost:5173/` or your production Vercel URL).
- Smoothly scroll down past the Paytm for Business-style hero section, highlighting the headline: *"Your Autonomous AI Business Partner"*.
- Hover over the 3 value pillars: **Real-Time Pulse**, **Grounded AI Actions**, and **Autonomous Team Execution**.

### 🎙️ Spoken Voiceover:
> "Every single day across India, millions of retail store owners, cafe managers, and merchants process hundreds of UPI and card transactions. 
>
> But here’s the harsh reality: today's POS terminals and billing software are completely passive. They record transactions, but they never tell a merchant what to do next. If revenue drops 30% on a Tuesday afternoon, traditional software just shows a red chart at the end of the month when it's already too late.
>
> Merchants don’t need more charts. They need a proactive business partner.
>
> That is why we built **GrowKaro** — India's first autonomous AI business partner that doesn’t just show data, but actively detects revenue opportunities, recommends high-impact actions, coordinates the store's staff, and measures real business results."

- Click **`[ Validate & Run Full AI Analysis ]`**.
- Show the **Data Quality Audit Report** (8 rows, 7 valid transactions, 1 flagged anomaly).
- Scroll through the isolated dataset analytics: Total Revenue (₹1,630), Top Categories, and Customer Breakdown.
- Open the **Scoped Real-Data AI Copilot**:
  - Type: *"What was our total revenue and best-selling product?"*
  - Show the Groq AI streaming answer citing the exact mathematical figures (₹1,630, Cold Brew).
- Click **`[ Clear Test Session ]`** to show non-destructive isolation.

### 🎙️ Spoken Voiceover:
> "Now, one of GrowKaro’s most powerful capabilities: **Test With Real Data**.
>
> Any merchant can upload their own real-world CSV export from any POS — whether it’s Pine Labs, Petpooja, Square, or Shopify.
>
> Our pure JavaScript parser reads standard CSVs, auto-detects column headers, and performs a complete data quality audit with zero silent failures.
>
> When we run analysis, GrowKaro generates an isolated, temporary **Dataset Session**. It calculates exact deterministic metrics — revenue, transactions, customer profiles — and runs our AI intelligence specifically on the merchant's uploaded data without touching the core database.
>
> You can even query the **Scoped AI Copilot**, which references only the verified mathematical calculations from this file to answer merchant questions with zero hallucinations.
>
> And when done, clearing the session leaves the store benchmark data 100% untouched."

## Pro-Tips for Recording Your Video

1. **Screen Resolution**: Set your monitor / browser to **1920x1080 (1080p)** with **100% display zoom** for crisp typography.
2. **Browser State**: Run `npm run demo:reset` right before recording so Cafe Aroma is in the pristine presentation state (1 notification, 1 pending action, clean tasks).
3. **Cursor Visibility**: Move your mouse deliberately; pause for 1 second over key badges before clicking.
4. **Audio**: Speak at an energetic, confident pace (~130–140 words per minute).
